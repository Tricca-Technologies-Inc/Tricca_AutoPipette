# Independent Review — AutoPipette Axis Expansion Plan, Rev I

Reviewer: independent agent (did not author the plan). Date: 2026-10-21.
Package reviewed as on disk at `/home/claude/autopipette-plan/` (identical to `/home/claude/autopipette-plan-revI.zip`; includes `phase0-survey.md` and the tidied verification register).

## 1. Verdict

**Not ready to approve as written. Approve with fixes once B-1 is closed.** The package is careful, internally well cross-referenced, and the parts I could reproduce do reproduce: the harness passes 27/27 in both stages, all three Klipper Kconfigs survive `olddefconfig` and build at the pin, and the rebuilt `klipper.dict` files are byte-identical to the ones supplied. But the collision interlock's core invariant (R11) can be defeated by ordinary, documented Klipper commands that move the toolhead without going through `G1`. Those are `RESUME` (reachable from tapd's own pause/resume RPC and from Mainsail's Resume button), `RESTORE_GCODE_STATE … MOVE=1` and `SET_GCODE_OFFSET … MOVE=1` (Mainsail's Z-babystep buttons). I reproduced all three in the pinned Klipper. Several docs also claim the reverse ("G92 and SET_GCODE_OFFSET can't fool the check"). Beyond that there are 17 "should" items, mostly phase-gating contradictions, two smaller interlock/homed-state gaps, missing issue rollbacks, and a conflict with the plan's own rule against putting MCU/CAN IDs in a repo. Phases 0–2 (survey, firmware builds, bench prep) don't depend on any of this and could start now.

Count: **1 blocker, 17 should, 13 nit.**
