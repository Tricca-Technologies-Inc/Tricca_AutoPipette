# Addendum to Rev L — Owner decisions of 2026-10-26

**Read this first.** It overrides the Rev L package in this directory wherever they disagree. The package itself is committed unchanged. These decisions come from a planning/grilling session with the owner (James) on 2026-10-26, held while turning the plan into GitHub issues. Decision IDs continue from umbrella §5.2.

## Decisions

| ID | Decision | Effect on the plan |
|----|---|---|
| D35 | **The EBB42 CAN toolboard is deferred.** The pipette plunger (`pipette_stepper`) and servo (`pipette_servo`) stay on the M8P, on today's pins. The M8P stays a USB MCU: no USB-to-CAN bridge firmware, no `can0`, no `[mcu EBBCan]`. | Topology becomes **M8P (USB) + Octopus V1.1 F446 (USB) + host MCU**. Requirements R3 (EBB part), D3, D6 and D29 (servo converter move) and Phase 5 (CAN bring-up, M8P reflash) are suspended. The EBB work is filed as separate deferred issues (`configs: move pipette to EBB42`, `cb1: EBB42 + M8P CAN bridge`, `cb1: can0`) so it isn't lost. |
| D36 | **The P axis stays in scope**, with the plunger on the M8P. `pipette_stepper` is still bound to G-code axis P by `plate-macros.cfg`; the binding doesn't depend on the EBB. | Stage 1/2, `G28 P`, TAP-5/6/7, CFG-3 and CB1-7 are unchanged, except that stage-2 limits go on `[manual_stepper pipette_stepper]` in `tricca-autopipette.cfg` instead of `ebb-pipette.cfg`. |
| D37 | **Named drawer stops.** Each drawer has any number of named stops along its travel, both inside the machine (pipetting positions) and outside it. Outside stops are for operator load/unload, handoff to other equipment, and covered spots against evaporation. | New tapd issue **TAP-12**. TAP-4 moves drawers by stop name (mm still accepted, for commissioning). |
| D38 | Stops are defined in tapd's **`config/locations/*.json`**, in a `drawers` block next to `locations`/`plates`, per machine (local root) and loaded additively like the deck. Each drawer lists `stops` (name → mm) and a `pipetting` subset. | TAP-12. Example below. |
| D39 | **Klipper keeps a per-plate tip-safe list** in `_PLATE_CFG` (default `[0, position_max]`). Interlock rule I2 becomes: *every plate homed and within `park_tol` of one of its own tip-safe positions*. Klipper stays the authority. At connect, tapd refuses any stop marked `pipetting` that isn't on Klipper's list (within `park_tol`). | CFG-1 gains the list and C-tests for a non-end safe stop (accepted) and an off-list mid-travel position (refused). The umbrella §6.3 contract gains the list as a Moonraker-readable variable, and TAP-2 discovers it. Positions are duplicated between the two configs on purpose: Klipper's copy is the safety authority, and tapd validates its own copy against it. |
| D40 | **Deck locations may link to a drawer stop.** A location entry may carry `drawer` + `stop`. It keeps fixed XYZ as today, and tapd refuses to pipette at it unless that drawer is at that stop. Because protocols compile before running, tapd keeps a compile-time drawer-position model: seeded from live Klipper state at protocol start, updated by `plate_move`/`home` lines, snapshotted by `domain_state_snapshot`. | TAP-12 (enforcement); TAP-8 (validation finding). |
| D41 | **Command names:** `plate_move <plate> <stop|mm> [--speed]`, `plates_in`, `plates` (status). Homing goes through `home plates` / `home <plate>` (TAP-5). Plates are addressed by name (`plate1`…`plate6`); the axis letter is accepted as an unambiguous alias. | TAP-4. |
| D42 | **TAP-7's first use case:** draw the pre-aspirate air gap while Z descends to the well, and the post-aspirate air gap while Z rises out of it, each as one coordinated `G1 Z… P…` move. Air is drawn only while the tip is above the liquid, so the coordinated segment ends/starts at the well's clearance height, never inside the liquid. Opt-in. | TAP-7 is no longer `needs-info`. |
| D43 | Fully specified issues are filed `ready-for-agent` (plus `safety` where marked), overriding D32. Deferred issues are filed `needs-triage`. Per-machine hands-on work is one `ready-for-human` checklist issue per machine (nick, marie) in cb1-autopipette. | Issue filing. |
| D44 | The offline harness's `klipper.dict` files (M8P USB, Octopus F446, host) are **committed** under `Tricca_Autopipette_Configs/tests/dicts/`, each with the Kconfig and Klipper rev it came from. | CFG-2. |
| D45 | **"Parked" is dropped as a term** (owner, 2026-10-26). Rev L defined it as within `park_tol` of 0 or `position_max`; after D39 that is narrower than what I2 checks, so a plate at a mid-travel tip-safe stop would read "not parked" while the tip may descend. Say **"at a tip-safe position"** for the I2 condition, and **"in"**/**"out"** for the two ends of travel. | Umbrella §3 glossary, I2 wording, the `RESUME`/`RESTORE_GCODE_STATE MOVE=1`/`SET_GCODE_OFFSET MOVE=1`/`STEPPER_BUZZ` guards, `QUERY_PLATES`, refusal text (e.g. "mid-travel" becomes "not at a tip-safe position"), TAP-4's `plates` status field and TAP-9's messages all use the new wording. CFG-1 replaces the hard-coded 0/`position_max` checks (`_CHECK_Z_DESCENT` and the `G1` wrapper in `plate-macros.cfg`) with the tip-safe list. |

## Changes to the Rev L documents

- **Umbrella §6.1:** drop the EBB/CAN branch. `[mcu]` is the M8P over USB (`serial:` written by bootstrap), `[mcu octopus]` the Octopus over USB, `[mcu CB1]` unchanged.
- **Umbrella §6.2:** I2 reworded per D39, with "parked" replaced per D45.
- **Umbrella §6.3:** add the `_PLATE_CFG` tip-safe list (D39). `PLATE_MOVE POS=` targets a stop's mm value; Klipper knows no stop names.
- **Umbrella §7.1:** Phase 5 drops out. Phase 2 bench prep covers the Octopus only. Phase 6 installs the drawers and the Octopus, and the pipette isn't rewired.
- **WP1:** §3.2–§3.4, §9 (CAN part), E-02 and E-03 (CAN) and the CAN half of E-04 are suspended. E-04 keeps the Octopus by-id check. E-12 changes to a servo stall test on today's M8P wiring, with no EBB brownout or CAN criteria. The power budget loses the EBB.
- **WP2:** no `ebb-pipette.cfg`. `tricca-autopipette.cfg` keeps the pipette sections and only gets the `[homing_override]` replacement. The harness (and `make_fixture.py`) drops the EBB and uses three dicts. The Rev L results (55/55, 52/52) must be re-run on the EBB-free config.
- **WP3:** CB1-1 builds Octopus firmware only. CB1-2 is `flash-octopus` plus a hardened `flash-manta`, both USB. CB1-3 is deferred. CB1-4 detects two USB MCUs by chip name (`stm32h723xx` → `[mcu]`, `stm32f446xx` → `[mcu octopus]`). CB1-6's ADR is "Octopus V1.1 as second USB MCU".
- **WP4:** TAP-2 also discovers the tip-safe list. TAP-4 per D41 and blocked by TAP-12. TAP-7 per D42. TAP-8 adds the D40 finding. TAP-10 also covers kiosk stop editing. TAP-11 adds the drawer-stop terms. New TAP-12.
- **M-tests:** CAN-specific checks drop out. M-01/M-12 (pipette regression) still run, since the config moves even though the pipette doesn't move boards.
